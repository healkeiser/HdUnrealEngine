# Volumes

Volumes (Houdini build) render the `density` field, or the first field, of a Volume prim as a UE heterogeneous volume with `M_HdUEVolume` (albedo 0.8; density is extinction per scene unit). The field can be:

- an `OpenVDBAsset` pointing to a `.vdb` file, read with OpenVDB;
- an unsaved SOP volume in the Solaris viewport: `OpenVDBAsset` or `HoudiniFieldAsset` prims whose `filePath` is an `op:` SOP layer. hdUE takes the cooked geometry from Houdini's locked-geometry registry, or cooks the SOP;
- a `.bgeo` / `.bgeo.sc` file, which is where the USD Render ROP writes native Houdini volumes for husk.

From Houdini geometry, the VDB or Houdini volume primitive named after `fieldName` is used, else the `fieldIndex`-th one. Grids are densified over their active bounding box and point-sampled with a stride above 512 voxels per axis. A bound Substrate Builder volume material replaces `M_HdUEVolume` in Houdini's viewport ([volume materials](../substrate-builder.md#volume-materials)); other volume materials, tapered Houdini volumes and frustum VDBs are not translated.

**Emission.** The constant primvar `primvars:unreal:emissionField` on the Volume prim (Hydra passes a volume its primvars, not other attributes; Houdini detail attributes become them) names another of its fields (`temperature`, `flame`, ...) that emits light: UE's BlackBody of the field's value times `primvars:unreal:temperatureScale` (kelvin per unit, 1 by default), times `primvars:unreal:emissionIntensity` (1). The field is sampled at the density's voxels (nearest voxel, 0 outside its own grid). As in UE, nothing emits unless asked: UE's heterogeneous volumes emit what their material makes of an imported grid, and UE prescribes no field or mapping. Both modes render it.

Measured against Karma: in realtime, a fog's brightness is within about 15%. Volumes shadow the scene in both modes. In realtime this is UE's heterogeneous volume Shadow Casting (`r.HeterogeneousVolumes.Shadows`, on in this project), with one shadow projection fitted around the volumes (`r.HeterogeneousVolumes.Shadows.Cascades` 0: with Virtual Shadow Maps, UE's default single cascade left the volume's shadow map empty). As in UE 5.8, the shadow reaches lights shaded by UE's deferred light loop: distant lights, and local lights with `unreal:megaLights` off (MegaLights does not support heterogeneous volumes); a light also needs Cast Volumetric Shadow (`unreal:light:bCastVolumetricShadow`, on by default for distant lights). The Path Tracer casts it (optical depth 1.4 against Karma's 1.9) and includes multiple scattering. It re-voxelizes volumes for each frame; batch renders use the shading rate 1 and 512 MB that UE's docs recommend for cinematic renders (`r.HeterogeneousVolumes.{Frustum,Ortho}Grid.ShadingRate`, `.MaxBottomLevelMemoryInMegabytes`; 4 and 128 MB by default, which made silhouettes blocky).
